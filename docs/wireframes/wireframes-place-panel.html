1 On a place page, the layout's "Places near here" list collapses to this back link, and the panel ends with "Nearby places" (5). Showing both lists side by side duplicated content and pushed the cards down. Spec change: §7.4/§7.5 should say this. The link returns focus to the list item that opened the place.

2 Heading = risk name + level badge (bars glyph + word). The So What? sentence is still the largest text on the card, so the consequence-first order holds, and screen-reader users hear the level first.

3 Trend block: dashed surface, "Projected" badge before the sentence in the DOM. It reads as a different kind of statement at a glance, without colour.

4 Low risks collapse to one disclosure button (aria-expanded). The panel stays about the risks that are flagged.

5 The map is one tab stop with its keyboard help visible; pins are hidden from assistive tech. The legend is text, and states that unflagged places are hidden (closes review gap 3).

2 Sea card last, "Projected" in the level slot with the dashed-bar glyph. It follows the expanded heat card, so the order is today's content first, projection last.
