So What?

An interactive map of Los Angeles County that pins everyday places (bus stops, parks, playgrounds, schools) and says, in one plain sentence, what each climate risk there means for a person standing at that place.

It covers air pollution, heat and wildfire from current data, plus sea level rise as a projection. It's a CS50x final project in progress, and these pages show the design decided so far, before the map is built.

Every place, number and sentence in these mockups is illustrative, not real data.
